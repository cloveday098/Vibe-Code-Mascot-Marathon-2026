# Vibe-Code-Mascot-Marathon-2026

## Challenge theme: Mascot Mayhem: The Rivalry Run
**Rules:**
Your school’s mascot has been pulled into a four-level adventure across a wildly reimagined version of campus. To save the school’s spirit trophy, the mascot must race, jump, and outsmart obstacles—then face the rival school’s mascot in a final showdown.
Make it an original game inspired by classic side-scrolling platformers; use your own characters, art, names, and sounds rather than copying existing game assets.
Core requirements
    • One playable hero inspired by your school mascot. 
    • Four levels with a clear beginning, middle, and finish: 
        
1. Campus Dash: Include at least two recognizable campus details, such as a library, mascot statue, quad, or dining hall. 
2. Themed challenge: A new setting or obstacle type—perhaps a gym, underground tunnel, or stormy sports field. 
3. The gauntlet: Combine challenges from earlier levels and introduce one new twist. 
4. Rivalry showdown: End with a boss encounter against the rival school’s mascot.
• At least one collectible power-up that visibly changes what the hero can do. 
• A way to win and a way to fail, with clear feedback to the player. 
• A playable, testable build that another team can try.
