# Vibe-Code-Mascot-Marathon-2026

## Challenge theme: Mascot Mayhem: The Rivalry Run
**Rules:**
Your school’s mascot has been pulled into a four-level adventure across a wildly reimagined version of campus. To save the school’s spirit trophy, the mascot must race, jump, and outsmart obstacles—then face the rival school’s mascot in a final showdown.
Make it an original game inspired by classic side-scrolling platformers; use your own characters, art, names, and sounds rather than copying existing game assets.
Core requirements
    • One playable hero inspired by your school mascot. 
    • Four levels with a clear beginning, middle, and finish: 
        
1. Campus Dash: Include at least two recognizable campus details, such as a library, mascot statue, quad, or dining hall. 
2. Themed challenge: A new setting or obstacle type—perhaps a gym, underground tunnel, or stormy sports field. 
3. The gauntlet: Combine challenges from earlier levels and introduce one new twist. 
4. Rivalry showdown: End with a boss encounter against the rival school’s mascot.
• At least one collectible power-up that visibly changes what the hero can do. 
• A way to win and a way to fail, with clear feedback to the player. 
• A playable, testable build that another team can try.


## Chance's Prompt:
Given the below description, create 

**Mechanics:** The Maryville College Scotsman is riding a tiger. He can jump, block attacks, and has tow attacks: sword thrust and bagpipe blunder. Make a platformer game in the style of older Nintendo games with the Scotsman riding the tiger as the playable character. The Scotsman (Scot) and his tiger (Aris) must navigate through four levels across the Maryville College campus to Gatlinburg.


Level 1: Maryville College
	* The level starts with the character jumping from the Anderson Bell Tower and fighting human enemies in suits that act similar to stompable goombas from the Mario games.
	* The character will pass by the College's famous Coveneant Stone in the background and finish the level by leaving campus and passing the MC sign with a Scottie dog on top of it.

Level 2: Driving on highway through Gatlinburg
	* This level will see the playable character jumping on and over cars along a highway up to Gatlinburg, TN.
	* Some cars will act erraticly and try smoosh you to represent unhinged TN drivers. The level ends by arriving at a hotel with big letters, saying "ACM Conference TODAY!"

Level 3: ACM conference (falling projectors and chairs)
	* This level will navigate through an old hotel with falling projectors and angry ACM members that you must jump on to eliminate.
	* The level ends by jumping dwn an elevator shaft.

Level 4: AI final boss (giant brain that launches different attacks branded with different AI company logos
	* Make an epic final battle against an AI final boss that somewhat resembles the MotherBrain from Metroid.

Important Notes:
• At least one collectible power-up that visibly changes what the hero can do.
• A way to win and a way to fail, with clear feedback to the player.
• A playable, testable build that another team can try.
• Each move and ability should have a button for the user to easily control the playable character
